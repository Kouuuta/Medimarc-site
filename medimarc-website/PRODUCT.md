# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Hospital procurement managers, clinic administrators, and healthcare facility buyers in the Philippines looking for reliable medical supply distributors. They need clear product catalogs, professional trust signals, and easy contact.

## Product Purpose

Medimarc Trading connects global medical manufacturers (Nipro) with Philippine healthcare providers. The website exists to showcase product categories, communicate credibility (15+ years), and generate sales inquiries.

## Brand Personality

Trustworthy, precise, caring. A medical-grade brand — clean, professional, no gimmicks.

## Anti-references

- Overly flashy "startup" aesthetics (gradient text, glassmorphism as default)
- Dark patterns or aggressive CTAs
- Generic stock-photo-heavy medical sites that feel cold

## Design Principles

1. **Clarity first** — healthcare buyers need to find products fast; navigation and categorization must be obvious
2. **Trust through restraint** — clean whitespace, precise typography, no decoration without purpose
3. **Professional warmth** — blue/teal palette feels clinical but approachable; imagery shows real medical context

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- WCAG AA minimum (4.5:1 contrast)
- Respect prefers-reduced-motion
- Touch targets ≥44px
